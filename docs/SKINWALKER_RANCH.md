# Skinwalker Ranch inspiration

NHI Communicator was inspired by the radio-frequency experiments featured in HISTORY's **The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch**. The broadcaster describes an investigation using instruments to examine unusual events at the Utah property; its official articles also describe investigators monitoring energy and signals. These are descriptions of the television program and the investigators' reported observations. They are not independent validation of a signal's source. [Official show page](https://www.history.com/shows/the-secret-of-skinwalker-ranch), [HISTORY's account of selected experiments](https://www.history.com/shows/the-secret-of-skinwalker-ranch/articles/7-of-the-most-mind-bending-moments-on-skinwalker-ranch).

Sky HISTORY also publishes a clip describing a signal appearing during a ceremony at Homestead 2. The clip is relevant as an example of the program's interest in RF measurements, without establishing who or what produced the signal. [Official Sky HISTORY clip](https://www.history.co.uk/videos/a-mysterious-signal-appeared-the-moment-the-prayer-began-curse-of-skinwalker-ranch).

## Why this app has a 1.600 GHz preset

The project's creator wanted a simple experiment around 1.6 GHz with visible measurements and readable prime-number packets. The app therefore uses **1.600 GHz as its laboratory tuning preset**. That number is an application setting. It is not evidence of NHI, an authenticated communication channel, a reconstruction of the show's measured waveform, or a guarantee that a response will occur.

The transmitted waveform is fully specified in this repository: two-frequency FSK carrying an ASCII laboratory packet. The receiver searches for that specific frame. It does not search for a general language, recognize an intelligence, or decode an unidentified signal merely because that signal appears near the preset.

## What an observation can tell you

| Observation | What the app establishes |
| --- | --- |
| A bright waterfall line | Received energy appeared in that frequency region during a displayed snapshot. |
| A line near the exact center | Energy or a receiver artifact is present; a DC spur is one possible cause. |
| A valid laboratory frame | The data matched this app's framing, checksums, and prime sequence. |
| A received copy of an outbound packet | Matching bytes were received; it may be a replay, echo, or compatible test transmitter. |
| No decoded packets | No matching complete frame was decoded during reception; this does not establish absence of all signals. |

A shielded conducted test evaluates the equipment and protocol inside the setup. It cannot demonstrate external contact. A claim about an unfamiliar signal's origin would require additional evidence, such as repeatable measurements, calibrated equipment, independent receivers, interference checks, and source localization.
